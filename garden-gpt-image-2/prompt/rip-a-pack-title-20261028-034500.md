# Rip a pack: title lockup brief (GPT Image 2.5 on Higgsfield, with two site screenshots as references)

## Shared brief (every direction)
Design the hero title lockup for the "Rip Lab" page of Crowns, a premium trading-card price tracker for Pokémon, Magic, Lorcana and One Piece collectors. The two reference images are screenshots of the real site: match its world exactly. Near-black and deep midnight-navy grounds, Crowns gold (#FFD24A, warm and polished), the crown logo's polished gold and sapphire blue, holographic trading-card foil, heavy condensed uppercase display type in the spirit of Anton, clean premium layout. This page is where you tear open a virtual booster pack, so the title must feel like the moment you rip the foil: exciting, a little dangerous, collectible.

Text: exactly "RIP A PACK". "RIP A" on the first line, "PACK" on the second line and larger. Every letter spelled correctly and clearly legible even small. No other words, no logos, no watermark, no signature.

Style: grungy and badass, never cartoonish, childish or Halloween-slime. Premium streetwear and skate-graphic energy crossed with trading-card foil culture. Heavy condensed caps leaning slightly forward, hand-finished edges, real wear (scuffs, creases, grit) used with restraint so it still reads expensive.

Output: the lockup alone, centred, on a transparent background (no backdrop, no plate behind it, no frame), wide horizontal composition with a clear margin, crisp detailed edges that will sit on a near-black web page.

## Directions
A. Torn foil: letters cut from a real crinkled booster-pack wrapper, silver-holographic foil with a rainbow sheen, a crimped serrated seal along the top of "RIP A". A jagged tear rips diagonally through "PACK" and the foil curls back to show glowing Crowns gold underneath. Thin black keyline, a faint gold spray mist, two or three short controlled gold drips off the bottom of PACK.

B. Gold graffiti piece: legible street-art letterforms sprayed in polished Crowns gold with chrome highlights and a deep navy 3D extrusion, crisp black outline, fine overspray, a few sharp drips, tiny holo-foil flecks in the fill like a refractor card. Tight letter-fitting, slight forward italic.

C. Collector's badge: heavy condensed letters in brushed, scuffed gold metal like a graded-card label, "PACK" torn open by three claw rips with sapphire-blue foil glinting inside the tears (the Crowns crown's sapphires), black keyline, light grunge, one bright specular glint.

D. Comic splash: bold condensed caps with halftone dots and ink-brush grit in white and Crowns gold, thick black outline, deep drop shadow, a torn-paper rip through the middle with a jagged edge, short speed streaks and small foil shards flying out, a couple of ink drips. Comic-book cover title quality.
