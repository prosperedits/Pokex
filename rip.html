Sealed products

Ranked by expected return on a rip.

Net resale is what the pulled singles would net you reselling them: live market less about 20% for fees and shipping, with bulk under $2 (commons, most reverses, plain rares) counted as $0 because it can’t be flipped at a profit. Return is net resale divided by what the sealed product costs. Profit odds is how often a rip clears its price after fees, across 20,000 simulated rips.
